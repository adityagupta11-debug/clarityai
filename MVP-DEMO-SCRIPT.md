# ClarityAI — MVP Demo Script (LinkedIn / X / YouTube Shorts)

> Format: **you on camera (small box, corner)** + **screen recording (main)**, side by side.
> Tone: confident builder showing real product, not a salesy ad. Talk like you're showing a friend.
> Target length: **60s primary cut**. A **30s hook cut** is at the bottom.

## DELIVERY NOTES (for the side-by-side recording)

- **Record screen and voice separately.** Do a clean screen capture first (no talking), then record your voiceover watching it back. Way easier to sound smooth, and you can trim dead air.
- **Pre-stage the data.** Have one already-analyzed interview ready so step 4 doesn't make viewers wait on real processing — either speed-ramp the loading (2–3s max) or cut straight to results.
- **Cursor = pointer.** Move deliberately and *pause* on each metric you mention. Fast cursor movement reads as nervous.
- **First 3 seconds decide everything.** The "most people bomb interviews not because of the answer, but how they say it" line is your hook — keep it tight, no throat-clearing intro like "Hey guys so today…".
- **Camera box:** bottom-right, ~20% of frame, so the app stays the star. Look at the lens, not your screen, on the hook and the closing line.
- **Captions on.** 80%+ watch muted. Burn in subtitles.
- **End on a CTA + the product**, not your face. Last frame should be the dashboard or logo with "Try it free →".
